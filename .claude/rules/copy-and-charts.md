# Copy, headings, and charts

Loaded when you touch `apps/web/**`, `packages/viz/**`, or `docs/**`. These restate `docs/DESIGN_PRINCIPLES.md` (the shared rationale is the `design-doctrine` skill); read the section itself when a rule below is not enough.

- **Objectivity is load-bearing.** Every factual claim carries a citation to a primary source (the agency of record). Every displayed number carries source, as-of date, and unit. Interpretations — "unsustainable," "crowding out," what a market move "means" — are attributed to named people or schools of thought, never asserted in Penny's voice. Test: every sentence survives being read aloud by any party it describes.
- **Neutral register in all narrative copy.** No editorial color, no imputed motives, no villains. Mechanics are facts; opinions are attributions.
- **Plain language everywhere** — the Money Stuff register. Short declarative sentences, plain words wherever precision survives, load-bearing terms defined inline. Never make a number wrong to make it friendly.
- **Headings lead with the finding, not the label.** Every chart or section heading states the observation, computed from the data beneath it where possible and written to self-retract when the data stops supporting it — never a generic label.
- **Charts:** fixed hues per series or entity, identity never by color alone, one accounting concept per chart (or a declared bridge), gaps render as gaps, honest axes, hover = tap, every figure cites its series and as-of date.
